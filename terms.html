Toolbox Vault
Effective Date: May 17, 2026  •  Last Updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Toolbox Vault mobile application (“Toolbox Vault,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the “App”) on iOS and Android devices. By installing or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and password (passwords are stored using one-way bcrypt hashing — we never see your plaintext password).
  • Tool & Inventory Data: Names, photos, model numbers, purchase dates, prices, locations, dealers, warranty info, receipts, and any content you choose to enter.
  • Dealer & Contact Info: Dealer business names, agent names, phone numbers, and email addresses you choose to add.
  • Borrower Info: Names and phone numbers of people you check tools out to — typed manually or imported from your device's Contacts with your explicit permission.
  • Photos & Receipts: Images you upload from the camera or photo library.
  • Subscription Info: Subscription tier, purchase date, renewal status (processed by Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment card details).
  • Feedback & Support: Any feedback or bug reports you submit through the in-app form.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs and performance data used to fix bugs. No personal content is included.
  • Aggregate Usage: Counts of which features are used, not tied to your identity.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location data of any kind.
  • Microphone audio.
  • Health, fitness, or medical data.
  • Browsing history outside the App.
  • Payment card or banking information (handled solely by Apple/Google).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core functionality.
  • Sync your inventory data securely across your devices.
  • Process and verify your subscription.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.
  • Detect, prevent, and fix technical or security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for targeted advertising. We do not share your data with data brokers.

3. Data Linked to Your Identity (Apple App Privacy)

For Apple App Store privacy nutrition labels, the following data is collected and linked to your identity (your account):

  • Email address (account creation)
  • User content (your tool inventory, photos, receipts, dealers, borrowers, notes)
  • Identifiers (an internal user ID and a RevenueCat anonymous identifier)
  • Purchase history (subscription status only)
  • Diagnostics (crash logs)

None of this is used for tracking, advertising, or shared with third-party marketers.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • All data in transit is encrypted with industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored using one-way bcrypt hashing.
  • Photos and receipts are stored on secure servers with restricted access.
  • We retain your data only for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days (some encrypted backups may persist up to 90 days before they cycle out).

  • Revoke any permission (Camera, Photos, Contacts, Face ID, Notifications) in your device's Settings.
  • Cancel your subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.
